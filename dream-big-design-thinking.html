1

Dream Big Mind Mapping

15 min
✦ Why we do this

Mind mapping mirrors how your brain naturally works — ideas branch out from one central theme instead of marching down a list — so in just minutes you surface more ideas, and more unexpected connections, and get the whole team's thinking visible on one page.

How it runs

  • 1Write “KOREA” in the center of the page — that's your star.
  • 2In 5 minutes, branch outward into 3–4 layers of ideas. Don't filter — go fast and let each branch spark the next.
  • 3Then share for 10 minutes — walk the group through your map and steal each other's best branches.

Example: one center, layer 1 = big themes, layers 2–3 = the specifics that spark ideas.

2

Define the Problem · 5 Why Root Cause

5 min
✦ Why we do this

Asking “Why?” five times drills past the obvious symptom to the true root cause — so the team spends its energy fixing the real problem instead of patching a surface issue that keeps coming back.

As a team, pick one real challenge

📉Clients don't use LI products well
💰C-Suite isn't willing to spend big
🧪Pilots stall and don't convert to big deals

Choose the one that stings most — then ask “why?” until you hit something you can actually act on.

Worked example · “Pilots don't convert”

  • ProblemPilots run fine but never convert into big deals.
  • ▼ why?
  • Why 1The pilot proves the product works — but not that it moves a business number.
  • ▼ why?
  • Why 2We never set success metrics tied to the client's own KPIs up front.
  • ▼ why?
  • Why 3We scoped it with end-users, not the economic buyer.
  • ▼ why?
  • Why 4We hadn't mapped what the C-suite actually cares about.
  • ▼ why?
  • Root cause →Discovery is anchored to features, not to the client's business outcomes & decision-makers.
3

Post-It Ideation

10 min

Everyone grabs a stack of post-its and writes ideas that could crack the root cause — one idea per note. Work silently and fast: the more the better, no judging yet. Quantity now, quality later.

Outcome-based pilot scorecard
Exec briefing before any pilot
Skills-data “aha” demo
Champion enablement kit
Quarterly ROI review with C-suite
Peer client story bank
90-day adoption playbook
+ your idea…
20+ Aim for at least 20 ideas as a group. Wild ones welcome — they often unlock the practical ones sitting next to them.
4

Group & Prioritize · Impact vs Effort

15 min

Share back, cluster the post-its into themes, then place each cluster on the grid. Where an idea lands decides what we do with it.

Quick Wins
High impact · low effort — do now.
Big Bets
High impact · high effort — plan & prototype.
Fill-Ins
Low impact · low effort — if time allows.
Time Sinks
Low impact · high effort — park it.

Plot every cluster — start with the Quick Wins, protect one Big Bet for step 5.

5
